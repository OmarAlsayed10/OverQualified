# About OverQualified

**OverQualified** is your ultimate AI-powered career assistant. Designed to help job seekers land their dream jobs, OverQualified combines intelligent resume building, deep AI resume analysis, automated job tracking, and career advice into one intuitive, easy-to-use platform.

Whether you're crafting your first CV, tailoring your resume for a specific job application, or tracking opportunities across top job boards, OverQualified guides you every step of the way.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Overview

### 1. 📝 Smart CV Builder & Templates
Create professional, ATS-friendly resumes in minutes.
- **Live Interactive Editor**: Build your resume with real-time visual previews.
- **Curated Professional Templates**: Choose from modern, executive, creative, and minimal designs tailored to your industry.
- **One-Click Section Ordering**: Easily rearrange sections (Experience, Education, Skills, Projects) and entries to highlight your strengths.
- **Smart AI Writing Support**: Instantly generate professional summary statements, punchy bullet points, and relevant skill suggestions with a single click.
- **Instant High-Quality PDF Export**: Download your finished resume as a crisp, perfectly formatted PDF ready to send to recruiters.
- **Multi-CV Management**: Store multiple versions of your CV in your account and set a primary resume for quick applications.

---

### 2. 📊 AI Resume Analysis & Quality Scoring
Get honest, actionable feedback on your resume before submitting it to employers.
- **Overall Quality Score (0–100%)**: Evaluated across key dimensions—Impact, ATS Compatibility, Structure, and Language.
- **Detailed Strengths & Weaknesses**: Immediate feedback showing what's working well and what needs improvement.
- **ATS Compatibility Checker**: Scans your resume for formatting issues or missing elements that might block Applicant Tracking Systems.
- **Personalized Interview Prep Questions**: Automatically generates relevant interview questions based on your resume and target experience.
- **"Ask AI About Your CV" Chat**: Chat directly with an AI assistant to ask specific questions about your resume and get instant improvement tips.
- **Downloadable PDF Report**: Export a complete analysis report to reference anytime offline.

---

### 3. 🎯 Vacancy Match & Resume Tailoring
Tailor your resume for any specific job posting to maximize your chances of getting an interview.
- **Job Description Match Score**: Paste any job description or vacancy link to see how closely your current resume aligns with the job requirements.
- **Skill & Keyword Gap Analysis**: See exactly which required keywords, skills, or qualifications are missing from your resume.
- **Readiness Roadmap**: Review partially evidenced and missing job requirements, prioritized by must-have status, before deciding how to strengthen your application.
- **One-Click Resume Tailoring**: Automatically optimize your resume's wording and bullet points to match the target vacancy without lying or distorting facts.
- **AI Cover Letter Generation**: Generate a tailored, professional cover letter specifically written for that job posting in seconds.

---

### 4. 📡 Job Radar & Application Tracker
Never miss a relevant job opportunity again.
- **Custom Search Preferences**: Set your target job roles, preferred locations, remote work preferences, experience level, and salary expectations.
- **Automated Opportunity Radar**: Scans global job boards (including Greenhouse, Lever, and top career portals) to discover fresh listings matched to your profile.
- **"Early Bird" Listing Alerts**: Spot newly posted jobs quickly so you can be among the first applicants.
- **Built-in Application Board**: Track your application status across stages (Matched, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Declined).
- **CV Variant Performance Tracking**: Keep track of which CV version was submitted to which job to measure response rates and optimize your approach over time.
